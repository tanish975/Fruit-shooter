## Fruit Archer 8.0 — the mobile rebuild

The browser prototype has been rebuilt from scratch as a portrait, touch-first Android game.

### Install
1. Download **FruitArcher-v8.0.0.apk** below on your Android phone (Android 7.0 or newer).
2. Open it and allow "install unknown apps" for your browser or file manager when asked.
3. Play. The game works fully offline and asks for no permissions beyond vibration.

### What's new
- **One-thumb slingshot aiming**: drag down anywhere, a dotted guide shows the arc, release to shoot.
- **Skewers**: arrows fly through fruit, so lining up several multiplies the score.
- **Perfect hits, combos and Fever**: centre hits pay double, streaks raise the multiplier, a full Fever bar doubles everything.
- **Three modes**: Time Attack (60 s), Survival (3 lives), Precision (15 arrows).
- **King Pumpkin** boss every third level, bombs to avoid, golden stars and gems to chase.
- **Five power-ups**: Triple Shot, Freeze, Blast Arrows, Double Points, Magnet Arrows, plus mode bonuses.
- **Progression**: coins, 7 upgrades, 5 bow skins, 12 missions, a daily bonus streak and lifetime stats.
- Haptics, synthesised sound and music, full-screen immersive mode, pause on app switch, Android back button support.

How to play

  • Drag down anywhere, like a slingshot. The dotted guide shows the arrow's path.
  • Release to shoot. Hit a fruit dead centre for a PERFECT (double points).
  • Arrows fly straight through fruit: line several up for a SKEWER multiplier.
  • Keep hitting to build your combo and fill the FEVER bar.
  • Never shoot the bombs. Do shoot the floating power-up bubbles.
